# Living Room & Loft

Create an online store website called "Living room and Loft" selling beds, wardrobes, sofas, and mattresses for the UK market. Feature free UK shipping, category browsing and product detail pages in GBP (£), a shopping cart, and a Cash on Delivery (COD) checkout flow that collects customer delivery and contact details without requiring online card payment.
